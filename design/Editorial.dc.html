Master of Public Administration · NASPAA-accredited since 1989

The management degree for public service.

Built around a working schedule

Evening classes, Monday–Thursday, 7:00–9:45 pm, in person and online, alongside a cohort of working professionals from agencies and nonprofits.

36 units: 21 of core coursework and 15 of concentration and electives in Human Resources, Public Finance, Local Government, or Public Policy. Most students take two courses a term and finish in three years.

A capstone seminar carries the comprehensive exam. No thesis.
