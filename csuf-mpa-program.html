Where an MPA can take you

The management track across public service.

  • City and county

    Management analyst, budget analyst, and city manager track.

  • Nonprofit sector

    Program director, development officer, and operations lead.

  • Health and human services

    Public health administrator, contracts, and grants manager.

  • Justice and public safety

    Agency administration, planning, and emergency management.

  • Education administration

    Campus and district operations and student affairs.

  • Special districts

    Water, transit, parks, regional planning, and policy.

Built around a working schedule

  • Evening classes, Monday–Thursday, 7:00–9:45 pm. A mix of in-person and online courses.
  • Three years part-time. Most students take two courses per term; students can complete the program in two years full-time.
  • A cohort of working professionals. Classmates bring current agency and nonprofit experience.
  • A capstone seminar, not a thesis. POSC 521 carries the comprehensive exam.

Core coursework

21 units

  • Foundations of Public Administration
  • Administrative Research and Analysis
  • Organizational Theory and Behavior
  • Public Budgeting and Finance
  • Human Resources Management
  • Capstone Seminar

Concentration and electives

15 units in Human Resources, Public Finance, Local Government, or Public Policy.
