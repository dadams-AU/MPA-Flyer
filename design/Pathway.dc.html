Your path through the MPA

  1. 1

    Talk to us

    Email mpaprogram@fullerton.edu or call (657) 278-3521.

  2. 2

    Apply

    By May 1 for a fall start, or November 15 for spring.

  3. 3

    Study in the evening

    Monday–Thursday, 7:00–9:45 pm, in person and online. 21 units of core coursework, then 15 of concentration and electives: Human Resources, Public Finance, Local Government, or Public Policy.

  4. 4

    Finish with a capstone seminar

    POSC 521 carries the comprehensive exam. No thesis. Three years part-time, two full-time.

  5. 5

    Move into management

    City & county, nonprofits, health & human services, justice & public safety, education, special districts. Alumni work at County of Orange, Yorba Linda Water District, City of Fountain Valley, California State University, and many more.
